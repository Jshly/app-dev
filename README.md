# 🌀 Jujutsu Kaisen

**_Jujutsu Kaisen_** is a **Japanese anime and manga series** by *Gege Akutami*.  
It tells the story of **Yuji Itadori**, a kind-hearted student who becomes host to **Ryomen Sukuna**, the *King of Curses*.  
To control his powers, Yuji joins **Tokyo Jujutsu High** under **Satoru Gojo**, fighting alongside **Megumi Fushiguro** and **Nobara Kugisaki** to defeat curses born from human fear and hatred.

---

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Yuji Itadori** – Brave and kind protagonist.  
- **Satoru Gojo** – His powerful mentor.  
- **Megumi Fushiguro** – Calm and strategic sorcerer.  
- **Nobara Kugisaki** – Fierce and confident fighter.
